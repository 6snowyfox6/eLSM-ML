Table I prints m_pi=138, m_K=496 as fit inputs. At c=0 the paper labels the lower pseudoscalar eigenvalue (138.0, = m_pi) as eta' and the upper (634.8) as eta; here compared as lower/upper eigenvalue. Finite-T part is QM without Polyakov loop -> not runnable (feature).
